# Reference checklist: what to send

Everything here calibrates the look and behaviour of the storm. You do not need all of it; tick what you have.
For each item the ideal is **3-10 stills or short clips**, with the **date, rough time (local), where you were
and which way you were facing** (a text file or filenames like `2022-05-23_1845_facing-W.jpg` is enough). Video
beats stills for anything that moves (inflow tails, striations, overshooting tops, funnel rotation).

Send by pointing me at folders (chase-site folders are fine) plus a note like "Morton TX: the 6:30 pm clips are the
vault, 7:10 pm is the wedge". I will build the reference sheet from that.

## Part 2: features

### Updraft and top
- [ ] Towering cumulus to cumulonimbus: a sequence of the tower going up (time between frames matters)
- [ ] Overshooting top: dome above the anvil, from a distance; a clip if it was pulsing
- [ ] Anvil from far away (50+ miles): the oval shape, backshear upwind, how thin it gets downwind
- [ ] Anvil underside from beneath: layered ice, streaks, how it thins
- [ ] Mammatus: under the anvil, and the rarer kind on the flanking line or downwind edge
- [ ] Striated updraft / meso "barrel" or "stack of plates": from the inflow side and from the rear
- [ ] Corkscrew LP updraft
- [ ] Updraft tilt: the lean of the tower with shear (side views)

### Inflow
- [ ] Inflow tails (beaver tails): short ones, long ones, more than one, tails at different heights
- [ ] Inflow bands feeding the base from the east/south-east
- [ ] Inflow vault: the clear gap between the meso and the forward-flank rain, especially when it is deep
- [ ] Scud rising into the base, condensation in the inflow
- [ ] Flanking line: the stair-step towers trailing south-west

### Base and wall cloud
- [ ] Rain-free base: flat, laminar, how wide
- [ ] Wall cloud: small ones, huge ones, rotating (video), with and without a tail cloud
- [ ] Tail cloud feeding the wall cloud from the forward flank
- [ ] High-based wall clouds (LP / High Plains) vs very low ones (Dixie / Plains moist days)
- [ ] Bowl lowering vs blocky lowering vs ragged

### RFD and occlusion
- [ ] RFD clear slot cutting into the base; the horseshoe shape
- [ ] RFD gust front / the "bear's cage" from the inflow notch
- [ ] Occlusion: old meso drifting back, new base forming to the east (a sequence is gold)
- [ ] RFD surge curling dust or rain around the wall cloud

### Precipitation
- [ ] Forward-flank rain curtain from the inflow side
- [ ] Hail core: green/teal tint, white hail shaft, hail on the ground with a size reference
- [ ] HP rain-wrapped meso: what the "whale's mouth" and rain foot look like
- [ ] Shelf cloud / outflow dominant stage (how a supercell dies)
- [ ] Virga under an LP base

### Tornadoes (every shape you have, with the storm type noted)
- [ ] Rope
- [ ] Elephant trunk / cone
- [ ] Stovepipe
- [ ] Wedge
- [ ] Multi-vortex (video)
- [ ] High-based funnel that never reaches the ground
- [ ] Dust tube under a funnel that does not connect (dry, high LCL)
- [ ] Rain-wrapped tornado (what little you could see)
- [ ] Satellite tornado or anticyclonic tornado
- [ ] Genesis sequence: dust swirl to full condensation, with times
- [ ] Rope-out / dissipation sequence, with times
- [ ] Debris cloud and the inside of the funnel (anything close, including other people's footage you trust)
- [ ] Damage: what EF-rated damage you have seen in person (houses, barns, trees, poles, grain bins, vehicles)

### Sky and light
- [ ] Sunlit storm vs backlit storm vs after sunset
- [ ] Lightning-lit structure at night
- [ ] The colour of the hail core vs the rain core
- [ ] Dust/haze in the inflow (Plains) vs clean humid air (Dixie)

## Part 3: numbers and notes only you have

- For each tornado you saw: how long it was on the ground, how wide it looked vs what the survey said, how fast it
  moved, and whether it moved with the storm or deviated (left turn, stall, loop).
- Any day where the forecast parameters said one thing and the storm did another, and your guess why.
- Any non-public data: DOW / VORTEX2 access, RadarScope or GR2Analyst archives, mesonet logs, Spotter Network tracks.
- Other people's photos or video that you consider the best examples of a feature (links are fine; I will use them
  as references, not reproduce them).
